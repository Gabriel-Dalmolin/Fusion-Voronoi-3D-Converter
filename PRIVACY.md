# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **3D Voronoi Converter** handles user data.

## Overview

Voronoi Converter is an Autodesk Fusion add-in that converts solid bodies into three-dimensional Voronoi structures. All processing is performed locally on the user's computer.

## Data Collection

Voronoi Converter **does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information**.

The App does not collect:

- Personal information
- Contact information
- Autodesk account information
- Usage analytics
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Design files
- Telemetry

All geometry processing is performed locally within Autodesk Fusion.

## Data Sharing

Because the App does not collect user data, **no data is shared with third parties**.

The App does not use:

- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Tracking technologies
- Third-party SDKs that collect user information

## Data Retention

Voronoi Converter does not retain any user data. Since no personal or design data is collected, there is no information stored by the App.

## User Control and Data Deletion

Because Voronoi Converter does not collect or store user data, there is no personal information to delete or remove.

Users may uninstall the App at any time through Autodesk Fusion.

## Internet Connectivity

The App does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

If future versions include optional features that require downloading dependencies or accessing online services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
